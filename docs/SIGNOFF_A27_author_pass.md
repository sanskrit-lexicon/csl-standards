## 2. Substance flags carried (not fixed)

1. **Abstract vs §4 case count.** The abstract says "From a 250-case deterministic sample … The resulting corpus of 1,722 reports"; §4 states the corpus spans 256 cases (250 Western + 6 *kośa*), the memo's m2 correction. A referee reading only the abstract will multiply 250 by four lanes and not reach 1,722. A human should decide whether the abstract also says "256 cases (250 Western plus six *kośa* entries)".
2. **Abstract names two validators, §11 names three.** The abstract validates "against a SHACL profile (pySHACL) and a compiled TEI RELAX NG schema (jing)"; §11 adds the Saxon + ISO Schematron SVRL layer (256 Lex-0 entries, part of the 1,014 checks). The §14 command comment `validate-external # real RNG (jing) + SHACL (pySHACL)` has the same omission. Untouched (a claim and a code block).
3. **"Three export profiles" vs four generated outputs.** §3 stage 3 lists TEI archival, TEI Lex-0, OntoLex/FrAC and MDF; the paper counts Lex-0 as a baseline inside the TEI profile and says "three" in the preamble, abstract and §1. Internally consistent, but a referee may ask; a one-clause definition ("three profiles, TEI in two variants") would settle it.
4. **Two evidence-share figures rest on different corpora.** §4 gives the five evidence phenomena as 69% of the 1,722-report corpus (post-MDF); §12 says the scale-stability check kept "evidence-loss share … about 70%" — that check predates the MDF lane (69.7 / 69.9% per the memo). Both are correct; §12 does say the check covers TEI, OntoLex and neutral only, but the reader has to connect the two sentences.
5. **Rounding.** §4 status split 58 + 37 + 4 = 99%; cause split 60 + 21 + 7 + 7 + 4 = 99% plus two "<1%" classes. Sums to 100 only with rounding; worth one word ("rounded") if a referee is strict.
6. **FRI primary-source entry.** "Frish Sanskrit Reader (CDSL/Cologne edition, 2015). (FRI)" carries no URL, editor or place, unlike every other primary source; verify the expansion and the 2015 date against the CDSL dictionary registry before submission.
7. **Coward & Grimes (2000) and the Saxon tool entry** have no URL/DOI; the references paragraph promises "Every entry is a real, verifiable publication or specification". Add a locator or soften the promise.
8. **Closing hub byline.** The manuscript now ends with `_Dr. Mārcis Gasūns_` (added by the 05-09-2026 mechanical header pass) while the academic byline sits under the title. For a submission copy the closing line must go; in the repo it is the house convention. Not removed here.
9. **Relative links remain** in the preamble (`[§14 Availability](#14-availability-and-reproducibility)`), §14 (`data/schema/`, `data/pilot/mdf/`, `data/pilot/figures/`) — untouched because a URL is substance under this pass; a camera-ready pass should make them absolute or drop them.
